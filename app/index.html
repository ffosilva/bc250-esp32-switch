BC250 Bluetooth adapter
While the machine is off, this device answers as the BC250's Bluetooth adapter so your paired controller wakes it.
Find it on the BC250:
Linux: bluetoothctl show (the Controller line)
Windows: Device Manager → Bluetooth adapter → Properties → Advanced
Select your controller
Turn on a controller that is already paired to the BC250 (it shows as paired), or put one in pairing mode (it shows up after a scan).
